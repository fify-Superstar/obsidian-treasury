Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

1. Overview

Obsidian Treasury (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, Founder's Circle checkout, waitlist, and related services, and how we use it.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact details — email address when you join the waitlist or complete a purchase.
  • Payment information — processed by Stripe. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.
  • Transaction metadata — checkout session details used to send confirmation emails.
  • Technical data — basic logs related to site hosting and serverless functions (e.g. request timestamps).

3. How we use information

  • To process Founder's Circle purchases and deliver confirmation emails.
  • To manage waitlist communications about product updates.
  • To operate, secure, and improve our website and services.
  • To respond to support or legal requests when required.

4. Service providers

We use trusted third parties to operate core flows:

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Resend — transactional email delivery.
  • Formspree — waitlist form submissions.
  • Netlify — hosting and serverless functions.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies and our contractual arrangements with them.

5. Data retention & security

We retain personal data only as long as needed for the purposes described above, or as required by law. Secrets and payment credentials are stored in environment variables / provider vaults, not in client-side code. Transmission uses HTTPS.

6. AI & training

Customer financial data is not used to train public or general-purpose AI models. Any future aggregated insights would require explicit opt-in and anonymization.

7. Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us. Waitlist subscribers may unsubscribe via the instructions in our emails.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at privacy@obsidiantreasury.com (or your preferred support email).
